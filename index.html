04 / Our story

A pantry built on repeat customers.

Sanmita Globals began behind a modest counter in Vidhyaranyapura with a simple idea: sell the kind of nuts and spices you would serve your own family.

We taste before we buy, sort before we pack, and refuse anything that needs a clever description.

  • Small-lot sourcing, graded by hand
  • Packed in Bengaluru, never over-seasoned
  • The same quality for retail and wholesale
